## 🔬 Research

### Real-Time Fraud Detection in Digital Payments

My current research investigates **machine learning methods for identifying fraudulent digital-payment
transactions**, where class imbalance, temporal drift and explainability matter as much as raw accuracy.

| Research Interests | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Fraud detection & imbalanced classification | Fraud is rare — accuracy alone is misleading |
| Classical ML & ensemble methods | Strong, interpretable baselines before deep learning |
| Explainable AI & model evaluation | Decisions must be auditable for real stakeholders |
| Behavioral & temporal transaction features | Fraud patterns evolve and are time-dependent |
| Performance vs. operational trade-offs | Precision/recall costs differ in production |

> **Goal:** evaluate models carefully across datasets and understand not only predictive performance,
> but also explainability and practical limitations.

**Status:** 🔬 Research and experimentation in progress.
